By hand

  1. Download the archive

    The button above or the releases page on GitHub.

  2. Run install.sh

    Unpack it and run ./install.sh. No password needed.

  3. Open it from the menu

    ninari shows up in your app menu and as the ninari command.

The app installs updates itself with its «Обновить» button. To remove: ./install.sh --remove; your library stays.

What it needs

System
Linux x86_64 with glibc 2.44 or newer: Arch, openSUSE Tumbleweed and the like. Flatpak will cover the rest. Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
Graphics
Any. HQ upscaling wants a GTX 1660 or better, Ultra and ArtCNN an RTX 3070 class card.
Window
On Windows and on Linux with Wayland the video plays inside the app's window; on X11 it opens in an mpv window of its own.
Internet
For the catalogue and episodes. Your library and progress stay on your computer.

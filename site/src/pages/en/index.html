Where episodes come from

ninari keeps no video. Episodes are found by plugins you add yourself: AniLibria, AnimeGO, AnimeLib, Anixart, Rutube, Shikicinema and YummyAnime.

FAQ

Do I need an account?

No, everything stays on your computer. Shikimori is only needed to keep your list there up to date.

Which systems does it run on?

Linux: Arch, openSUSE Tumbleweed and other systems with current libraries. Windows 10 and 11: an installer or a portable version. Flatpak for any distribution and AUR are next, then macOS.

What graphics card does it need?

Any. HQ, Ultra and ArtCNN prefer a discrete one; the level changes right in the player.

The dub I want is missing?

None of your sources has it. If a source found the wrong title, fix it with "Wrong title?".

What language is the app in?

Russian for now; an English interface comes later.

Is it free?

Yes, with no ads or subscriptions. The code is open on GitHub.

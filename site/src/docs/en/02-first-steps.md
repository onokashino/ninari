# First steps

## The first start

On the first start ninari walks you through three steps; each can be skipped and set up later.

1. **Источники серий** (episode sources). **Подключить все** (add all) installs the plugins from the official catalogue. Without them the app shows the catalogue but finds no episodes.
2. **Список с Shikimori** (your Shikimori list). Enter your nickname and the titles of your public list join your library with their statuses and watched episodes.
3. **Апскейл Anime4K** (upscaling). Pick an upscaling level for your graphics card. More in [Upscaling](upscaling.html).

## Find and play

1. Open **Поиск** (search) and start typing a name, in any keyboard layout.
2. On a title's page, pick a dub in the list next to **Серии** (episodes). Each one shows its best quality and how many episodes are out.
3. Click an episode or the **Дальше** (next up) card.

The title then sits on the home screen with **Продолжить** (continue), which opens the same minute of the same episode in the same dub.

## Your library

The button next to **Продолжить** sets a status: **Смотрю** (watching), **В планах** (planned), **Просмотрено** (completed), **Отложено** (on hold) or **Брошено** (dropped). **Оценить** (rate) gives a score from 1 to 10 and a note for yourself.

The **Библиотека** (library) page sorts your titles by status; you can search and sort them there.

## Handy

- <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> opens quick navigation: sections, your titles and the catalogue from the keyboard.
- **Каталог** (catalogue) has ready collections, filters by genre (including "without this genre"), studio, year and season, and a random title.
- **Расписание** (schedule) lists the week's episodes in your time zone; **Только мои** (mine only) keeps the titles in your library.

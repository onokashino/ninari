# Docs

ninari gathers anime episodes from many sources into one app: every dub with its quality, an mpv player that sharpens the picture, the Shikimori catalogue and progress that never gets lost.

> The app speaks Russian for now. These pages give each button's Russian label as it appears in the app, with its meaning next to it.

## Using ninari

- [Installing](install.html): one command, updating and removing.
- [First steps](first-steps.html): sources, your Shikimori list, the first episode.
- [Player](player.html): controls, keyboard shortcuts, skips and sound.
- [Upscaling](upscaling.html): Anime4K and ArtCNN, which mode to pick.
- [Plugins](plugins.html): where episodes come from and adding your own catalogue.
- [Sync](sync.html): your Shikimori account and your own devices at home.
- [Search by frame](frame-search.html): find an anime from a screenshot.
- [Questions and problems](faq.html): what to do when something is off.

# Sync

## Shikimori

**Настройки → Shikimori → Войти через Shikimori** (Settings → Shikimori → Sign in with Shikimori) opens the browser. Once you sign in, statuses, watched episodes, scores and notes go to your list on Shikimori too.

Without signing in you can still bring your list over: **Импорт списка** (import a list), enter a nickname. The titles of the public list are added with their statuses and episodes; what's already in your library is left alone.

## Your own devices

ninari syncs the library, watched episodes, your place in an episode and dubs between your computers on one network. No cloud: the devices talk to each other directly, and the data is encrypted.

1. On both computers: **Настройки → Устройства → Синхронизация в локальной сети** (Settings → Devices → LAN sync).
2. On the first one, press **Показать код** (show code). The code works once and lasts a few minutes.
3. On the second: **Связать устройство** (pair a device), enter the code.

From then on changes travel by themselves; **Синхронизировать сейчас** (sync now) starts a round at once. **Отвязать** (unpair) breaks the link.

## A copy of the library

**Настройки → Данные → Сохранить копию** (Settings → Data → Save a copy) puts your list, statuses, dubs and episodes in one file. **Восстановить из копии** (restore) adds back only what's missing.
